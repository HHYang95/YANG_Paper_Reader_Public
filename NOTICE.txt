YANG Paper Reader: standalone public edition
Notice: operation, data use and limitations

Read this before you install the Reader. LICENSE.txt sets the terms of use;
INSTALL_GUIDE.md takes you through the installation.

2. What is sent where

   To your AI provider, through your own account. The Reader uses the
   Claude Code or Codex program installed on your PC, signed in with your own
   account. Anthropic (for Claude Code) or OpenAI (for Codex) receives each
   request under your account's terms, and the use counts on your account.
   The Reader stores no sign-in details.
   - While Start the Paper Summary by itself is on in General Settings, as it
     is at first, the Reader sends a Paper Summary request by itself for a PDF
     for which no Paper Summary was tried and none exists: when you open it,
     at its first open or a later one; when you turn the switch on while it is
     open; and right after Reset sets aside its reading records that could not
     be read. Each such request sends the text of every page, without page
     images.
   - While the switch is on, after the full parsing, the Reader sends by
     itself one Summary Update request for the paper's newest Paper Summary,
     once for each parsing, also where that Paper Summary was made with Make
     Paper Summary before the full parsing. A Paper Summary made with Make
     Paper Summary after the full parsing, which read the parsed text already,
     is not updated for that parsing; one the Reader made by itself is updated
     also where it was made after the full parsing. Turning the switch on can
     start the Summary Update for the open paper as well. The Summary Update
     checks that Paper Summary and fills what it lacks: it sends the parsed
     text of every page together with that Paper Summary, and the image of
     each Figure that a note of the Paper Summary names (and of such a Table
     or Equation whose parsed text is unclear). Once a paper's Retry Paper
     Summary (item 4) has been used, the Reader sends no Summary Update by
     itself for that paper.
   - With the switch off, the Reader sends neither by itself.
   - Make Paper Summary, offered where no Paper Summary of the paper is
     completed, queued or running, sends one Paper Summary request on each
     press: the parsed text of every page after the full parsing, and
     otherwise the text of every page, without page images in either case.
   - Translate sends the text you selected together with its image cut from
     the page. Ask on a selection sends your question and the text you
     selected: before the full parsing also its image and the paragraphs
     around it, as text and images; after the full parsing the parsed text
     around it and the image of a Figure it lies in or names (or of such a
     Table or Equation whose parsed text is unclear).
   - A Section's Summary sends the text of that section. Ask on a section
     sends its parsed text and the image of each Figure in it or named in
     it (and of such a Table or Equation whose parsed text is unclear).
   - The actions of a Figure, Table or Equation (Ask, LaTeX, Export Data)
     send the element's image cut from the page with its caption and the
     paragraphs that refer to it, as text and images. Ask and LaTeX add the
     paragraphs around it, and LaTeX adds, after the full parsing, the
     Equation's parsed text.
   - On a card in Assistance & Notes, Continue Conversation, New
     Conversation and Continue Research send your question (Continue
     Research a set question) with the earlier turns, or with the earlier
     answer or the note the conversation starts from, and the source of
     that record from the PDF; for the paper's Summary that source is the
     text of every page. Retry sends a failed or stopped request again.
   - A request of Translate, Ask, Export Data or a conversation may add one
     more request with more source from the same PDF, which can include
     whole pages of the PDF as images.
   - Auto Highlight sends the full parsed text of the paper for each pass.
   - HTML Overview uses the locally parsed paper and the Paper Summary the
     Reader has saved for it, which your AI wrote, and makes no AI request.
     Saving it sends nothing; its folder holds the page, its data, images cut
     from the paper's pages and a copy of KaTeX's style, fonts and license,
     which draw the page's formulas, and the page loads nothing from the
     internet.
   - When the Reader checks which program version is installed, it sends
     nothing.

   To public lookup services, for Paper Information. When a PDF opens, when
   the layout or the parsing completes the paper's details, when you open
   Paper Information and when you click Refresh in it, the Reader sends the
   paper's DOI to Crossref (api.crossref.org). Where the first page and the
   PDF's details hold no DOI, it sends the one DOI printed on the last page
   instead, and without any DOI the title, the first author and the year.
   After the full parsing, a paper with a title and no DOI whose PDF gives
   neither its authors nor its year, and that Crossref did not match, is
   looked up once more when the parse read an author and a year on its
   first page: the Reader then sends Crossref the title with that first
   author and that year. This also holds where the last page prints a DOI
   that Crossref does not know or that belongs to another title, and for a
   PDF whose only author in its details is a PC account name, such as
   Admin, which counts as no author. This lookup is not made again once it
   has an answer.
   Once Crossref matches the paper, the journal's name goes to
   lab.peptide.ink for the Impact Factor and quartile. No other text of the
   paper is sent.

   Nothing, for parsing. The page layout and the full parsing run on your PC
   with the models you install. Nothing of the paper is sent for them.

   Links you click, in a PDF or in Paper Information, open in your web
   browser. A click on the homepage in About, in the Information dialog,
   opens its one fixed address, https://hhyang95.com, in your web
   browser.

   User Manual, in the Information dialog, and User Manual and Install
   Guide, in the Start menu, open in your web browser MANUAL.html and
   INSTALL_GUIDE.html, files installed with the Reader on your PC. They send
   nothing, and a web address in those pages opens only when you click it.

   You download the Setup from this repository's GitHub Releases, in your
   web browser or with the two PowerShell lines of INSTALL_GUIDE.md, which
   check its SHA-256 before they start it. GitHub may redirect asset
   downloads to its download infrastructure. The Setup sends no paper data
   and does not open the Reader or make an AI request.

   The Setup holds the program itself and downloads nothing. Its checks of
   the requirements run on your PC and send nothing, and a link on its
   Requirements page opens in your web browser only when you click it. The
   runtime setup that it runs at its end downloads what the next paragraph
   names.

   The runtime setup, which the Setup runs at its end and Set Up Parsing
   Runtime in the Start menu runs again, downloads the pinned Python packages
   from files.pythonhosted.org and paddle-whl.cdn.bcebos.com and downloads the
   two models from huggingface.co. Package hashes are checked locally against
   the lock file. Audit compares the local installation report with the shipped
   release catalog; Audit makes no network request. The runtime setup sends
   nothing of your papers. The model downloads start at huggingface.co and
   may be redirected to other download hosts that Hugging Face uses.
   Each model request names the program to the server with the text
   YANG-Reader-Model-Staging/1 (its User-Agent). The installers of Python,
   Claude Code and Codex that the guide names contact their own vendors.

   The Reader sends nothing to Hyeon-Ho Yang. A click on the homepage makes
   your web browser load that page from his site; the Reader gives the
   browser that fixed address alone and nothing of yours.


3. The wait for the page layout

   The first time you open a PDF, the page is dimmed and locked under
   Preparing Page Layout until the layout of every page is ready. Reading
   opens then. The full parsing goes on in the background (the footer reads
   Background Parsing) and ends with Tools Ready. If the layout cannot be
   prepared, for example because the parser cannot run on your PC, the PDF
   stays locked and the Reader offers Retry Layout and Open Another PDF.


4. Recovering a Summary after a runtime or sign-in failure

   Open the failed paper's Summary to see the reason. If the Reader can
   confirm a runtime or sign-in failure, fix the installation or sign in,
   then choose Retry Paper Summary. This sends one new request to your
   selected AI account, with the parsed text of every page after the full
   parsing and otherwise the text of every page, without page images. The
   earlier failure stays in your reading record.
   The retry is available once per PDF. Closing, reopening or resetting the
   reading view keeps that limit. Older records with insufficient evidence
   show their recorded reason. A saved summary remains available to read.


5. Reset deletes a paper's notes

   Reset, beside the Assistance & Notes title, deletes the paper's notes and
   highlights, its Explanation, Translate and other answers, its Auto
   Highlight set and the places of its answer boxes. It asks first (Reset
   Reading Records, with Cancel as the default). It keeps the Paper
   Summary, the 5-Sentences Summary, the
   PDF, the parsed source and your reading position.


6. The PC, the GPU and the AI runtime the Reader needs

   PC. Windows 10 version 1809 or later, or Windows 11, 64-bit (x64);
   Python 3.12, 64-bit; 4 GB of RAM; about 12 GB of free disk
   space for the program and the parsing runtime; and an internet
   connection during setup and for AI requests.

   GPU. The full parsing runs only on the first NVIDIA GPU of the PC, with
   CUDA. There is no fallback to the processor. The GPU needs compute
   capability 8.0 or higher, which supports bfloat16 (bf16), and NVIDIA
   driver 576.02 or newer. GPU memory of 12 GB or more is recommended: the
   maintainers of PaddleOCR-VL, the parsing tool, name 12 GB in their pinned
   FAQ as the smallest configuration they have run. The runtime setup checks
   that the GPU supports bfloat16 (bf16) and stops when it does not. These
   figures are minimums and a recommendation, not a guarantee for every
   paper or PC.

   AI runtime. Claude Code 2.1.330 or newer, or Codex 0.171.0 or newer,
   installed on your PC and signed in with your own account. The Reader looks
   in each program's installer folder, its npm global package and every
   folder of PATH, and AI Settings lists where it looked. Without one, the
   Reader still opens and parses PDFs, but refuses every AI request with the
   reason. A newer runtime is accepted, but it is not proven to work: an
   answer the Reader does not recognize stops that request with a plain
   reason.


7. Linked and network folders are not opened

   The Reader does not open a PDF in a linked or network folder (a folder
   moved with a junction, a SUBST drive, a mapped network drive or a network
   path). Copy the PDF to a local folder and open the copy. It also refuses
   to save images, exported data or an HTML Overview into such a folder. Keep
   the program folder and the runtime folder in ordinary local folders too.


8. Windows startup restrictions

   The Setup and the program carry no code signature. Windows may warn
   about or block an unsigned program. Where it shows "Windows protected
   your PC" for a Setup whose SHA-256 you have checked, choose More info,
   then Run anyway. While Smart App Control is on, Windows blocks unsigned
   programs, so the Setup and the Reader cannot run on that PC. If policy
   blocks it, keep the exact message and consult your administrator; do not
   disable organizational protections as an installation step.


9. Standalone edition

   This edition works on its own. PDF reading, local parsing, your AI
   account, notes and exports remain subject to the behavior and
   disclosures above.
