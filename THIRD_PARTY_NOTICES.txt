Independent installation script

install/YANG_Paper_Reader.iss, the Setup's script, is AGPL-3.0-or-later, with
complete source and license in the public repository. It is separate from the
protected Reader application and its small startup wrappers. See
install/LICENSE-SCOPE.md.

Separately acquired components

System Python, the NVIDIA driver, the parsing environment/models and Claude
Code/Codex are not bundled. Each keeps its own vendor/upstream terms. The
documented parser profile acquires locked PaddlePaddle GPU 3.3.1,
PaddleOCR/PaddleX 3.7.0, NVIDIA runtime packages, and fixed-revision
PaddleOCR-VL-1.6 and PP-DocLayoutV3 models. Installation guides identify their
sources and the user's account responsibility. This notice does not relicense
them or claim a complete installed-artifact audit of a user's environment.

Evidence limits

Original notices and pinned source companions are supplied without claiming
reproduction of upstream native binaries or every recursive build input.
FFmpeg interface-compatible replacement has not been tested here. The exact
PDF.js QuickJS-wrapper build commit is unproved; the project's original
notice is retained. Source/version correspondence and synthetic tests are
not a clean-machine, GPU, paper-accuracy or live-AI guarantee.
